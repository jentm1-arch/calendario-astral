How do I remove it?

iPhone: open any event of the astro calendar → at the very bottom, “Unsubscribe from this calendar”.
Android / Google: at calendar.google.com (on a computer or in “Desktop site” mode), under “Other calendars” hover or tap “My Astro Calendar” → ✕ → “Remove calendar”.
If the events went into your main calendar (by downloading the file), delete them one by one or leave them: they only last a month.
Your other events stay untouched.
